The concept

Galaxy clusters as gravitational telescopes

JWST Frontier Fields (JFF) is a community concept for a large JWST program on massive galaxy clusters that act as gravitational lenses. Each cluster magnifies the sky behind it, so deep observations through these fields reach sources intrinsically fainter than any blank field delivers in the same time.

The approach follows the Hubble Frontier Fields and is being prepared for the JWST Multi-Cycle Treasury opportunity through a series of community white papers.

Survey design

One architecture across cycles

The baseline couples NIRCam imaging with NIRSpec spectroscopy on each cluster, so every hour of spectroscopy also deepens the imaging. Repeated visits build an ultra-deep core and deep flanking fields over several years.

Community activity

Lens modelling challenge

Accurate magnification maps underpin every JFF science case. The community is preparing a coordinated lens modelling challenge for 2027, comparing independent mass reconstructions of the JFF clusters. Plans will be posted here as they take shape.
