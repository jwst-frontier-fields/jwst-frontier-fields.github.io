JWST Frontier Fields

A community concept for a JWST Multi-Cycle Treasury program targeting strong-lensing galaxy clusters.

Concept

JWST Frontier Fields (JFF) is a community concept for a large JWST program on massive galaxy clusters that act as gravitational lenses. Each cluster magnifies the sky behind it, so deep observations through these fields reach sources intrinsically fainter than any blank field delivers in the same time.

The concept follows the Hubble Frontier Fields, which paired deep imaging of six lensing clusters with a broad community effort. JFF would bring that approach to JWST, with NIRCam imaging and NIRSpec spectroscopy accumulated over multiple cycles.

JFF is being prepared for the JWST Multi-Cycle Treasury opportunity through a series of community white papers that set out the science cases and the survey design. This page lists the series, and the survey design will be posted here in more detail after the white papers are submitted.

Baseline design

The baseline couples NIRCam imaging with NIRSpec multi-object spectroscopy on each cluster. A wide mosaic first covers the cluster and two flanking fields. Later epochs place NIRSpec on one of the three fields while NIRCam observes another in coordinated parallel, so every hour of spectroscopy also deepens the imaging. Repeated visits at alternating orientations build up an ultra-deep imaging core and deep spectroscopy across all three fields over several years.

The quantitative design and the figures behind it will follow after the submission of the white papers.

Design options

Within this architecture, several choices remain open, including the cluster sample, the balance between area and depth, and the mix of instrument modes. The white papers examine these options against their science cases, and the adopted design will follow from that community input.
